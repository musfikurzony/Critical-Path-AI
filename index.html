How this works
Fabric Origin decides which regional calendar (CN or BD) governs fabric production days.
Production phases skip holidays so factories always keep their full working-day allotment.
Garments ETD always snaps to a Sunday sailing day, and steps 7 days clear of any Eid.
Fabric In-house avoids Friday, Saturday and BD holidays.
